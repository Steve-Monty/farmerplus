Country labels use Natural Earth 1:50m Admin 0 Countries, version 5.1.2.
Source: https://github.com/nvkelso/natural-earth-vector/blob/v5.1.2/geojson/ne_50m_admin_0_countries.geojson
Public domain: https://www.naturalearthdata.com/about/terms-of-use/

The bundled countries.json retains ADMIN labels and polygon coordinates and
adds polygon bounds for lookup. Generalised borders are contextual labels,
not legal boundaries. Small islands, coastlines and border locations may be
unavailable or approximate. No coordinates are sent to a geocoding provider.
