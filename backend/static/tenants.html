How commissions work

For a R100 course at 20% creator commission and a 10% platform share: the creator receives R80; the tenant receives R20 commission, with R2 immediately allocated to the platform; the tenant keeps R18. All entries commit together or none do.

FarmerPlus starts with a 0% platform share. Re-downloading a purchased course does not create another charge. Refunds reverse the original split, not today’s rates.

Course purchase records currently remain pending Moodle enrolment. They must not be presented as confirmed course access.
