Privacy policy

Effective 27 September 2026

This policy explains how FarmerPlus handles information when you use our mobile web app, administration services and connected Learning platform at agritec.earth. For privacy questions or requests, contact steve@informationcapital.co.za.

Information we handle

  • Account information: your name, email address, verification status and account identifiers. Our Keycloak sign-in service manages passwords, login sessions and account security.
  • Farm records: information you enter or upload, including farms, field boundaries, activities, production records, notes and attachments. Which records are present depends on the tools you use.
  • Location: coordinates and reported accuracy when you allow a location request for registration or farm mapping. Weather uses its own setting and is off on a fresh installation.
  • Learning: your linked learner identity and, where applicable, enrolments, course activity, progress and completion information managed by the Learning platform.
  • Settings and technical information: profile preferences, installed FarmerPlus tools, browser/device capabilities made available by your browser, app version, sync status and records needed to diagnose service or security problems. A web app cannot read all information on your phone or computer.

Why we use it

We use this information to create and secure your account, deliver the features you request, synchronise your records, restore access, connect your Learning account and operate and support the service. Account administration and service delivery rely on the information needed to provide those services; permission-based features, such as device location, use the choices available to you. We may also retain information needed to meet legal obligations or address misuse and security incidents.

Google and other sign-in providers

If you choose Google sign-in, Google supplies basic identity information, including your name, email address and Google account identifier. FarmerPlus uses this to authenticate you and maintain your account. We request the openid, profile and email scopes; we do not request access to your Gmail, Google Drive or contacts. FarmerPlus does not receive your Google password. Google also processes your sign-in under its own privacy policy. Other providers are available only when configured and shown on the sign-in screen.

Sharing and connected services

Our backend and Learning platform share the account identifiers and profile information needed to provide one connected account. Optional tools may synchronise the records needed for their features. Organisation and cooperative access is governed by configured membership, permissions and sharing choices; installing a tool alone does not make all your records public.

Service providers may process information needed for hosting, authentication, email delivery, maps or other features you choose. For example, SMTP2GO delivers authentication email, and a map provider receives network requests when online map data is loaded. Providers may process data in other countries. We do not use Google sign-in data to sell advertising profiles.

Location and your device

Your browser asks for location permission. You can refuse or revoke it in browser or device settings; features needing location may then be unavailable. Desktop coordinates may be approximate and are not survey measurements. FarmerPlus uses browser-provided capabilities and permission controls; it does not gain unrestricted access to your device.

Offline storage and cookies

FarmerPlus stores records, queued changes, preferences and downloaded learning content in browser storage to support offline use. Essential cookies maintain sign-in and protect authentication requests. A device-level offline unlock, if enabled, is separate from your online password and does not encrypt all browser storage. Protect access to shared devices.

Offline data belongs to a browser and website address. Clearing site data, browser eviction or moving to another address can make local copies unavailable. Synchronise or export pending work before clearing storage or changing devices. Signing out does not automatically erase saved records and downloads.

Retention and security

We keep information while needed to operate your account and requested services, and where needed for legal, security, audit or recovery purposes. Some information may remain in protected backups or necessary learning records after an account request. There is no single retention period for every record type. We use controls including HTTPS, restricted server access and authenticated sessions; no system can guarantee absolute security.

Your choices and requests

You can update available profile settings, manage optional features and review device permissions. Contact support to request access, correction, export or deletion of your information, or to raise a privacy concern. We may need to verify account ownership and explain any records we must retain or cannot change. Depending on applicable law, you may also have rights to object to processing, withdraw consent or complain to a data protection regulator.

Changes to this policy

We will publish changes on this page and update the effective date. Material changes affecting how we use your information will be communicated through the service or appropriate account contact channels.
